About Pirtak

Iran's Trusted Source for Calcium Carbonate & Industrial Ores

Pirtak Industrial Minerals is a leading Iranian mining and processing company specializing in high-purity calcium carbonate, limestone, dolomite, and related industrial minerals. With strategic quarry sites across Iran, we deliver consistent quality to domestic and international clients.

Our subsidiary, Sababehpar, focuses on micronized powder production and advanced mineral processing, ensuring that every shipment meets the exact specifications of industries like plastics, paint, construction, and agriculture.
